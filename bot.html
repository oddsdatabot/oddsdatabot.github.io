OddsDataBot contact

We are odds-data, a small odds research project.

User-Agent OddsDataBot/1.0 (+https://oddsdatabot.github.io/bot)

What we collect

We collect only public pre-match odds available while logged out: event, market, outcome, line, price and time. We use no sportsbook accounts and collect no personal data, page text or images.

The current UK pilot is for research only. We publish none of its data.

We make a few requests per book every several minutes and obey each site's robots.txt.

How to ask us to stop

To ask us to stop, open an issue naming your site. We stop all collection from your site as soon as we see the issue, and we confirm the stop in that issue.

You do not need an account to stop us. Any of these stops all collection from your site straight away, with no retries:

  • an HTTP 403 or 429 response to OddsDataBot;
  • a robots.txt rule for User-agent: OddsDataBot (or *) that disallows the pages we read. We re-read robots.txt at least once a day.
